The real problem

Most Nigerian websites look pretty. Almost none of them convert.

Spending money driving traffic to a confusing, slow website is the fastest way to burn your marketing budget. Here is what is usually going wrong:

Fails the 4-second test

A new visitor lands on your page. If they cannot tell what you sell, who it is for, and why buy from you within four seconds, they close the tab.

Checkout drop-offs on e-commerce

Your shop requires 12 checkout steps and fails when a card has a 3D-Secure timeout. Customers want smooth WhatsApp or bank payment confirmation.

The contact form dead-end

Inquiries get buried in an unmonitored inbox. By the time a sales rep replies 18 hours later, the client has already contracted another firm.

Mobile lag on Nigerian networks

Heavy uncompressed images that take 8 seconds to load on MTN or Airtel. Over 75% of your mobile traffic bounces before the page renders.

Zero organic Google visibility

Your site exists, but when someone in Lagos searches for your exact service, only your competitors appear on page one.
