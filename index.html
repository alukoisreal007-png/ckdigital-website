The real problem

Most websites look pretty. Almost none of them convert.

Spending money driving traffic to a confusing, slow website is the fastest way to burn your marketing budget. Here is what is quietly costing you revenue:

Leak 01

Fails the 4-second test

A new visitor lands on your page. If they cannot tell what you sell, who it is for, and why to choose you within four seconds, they leave and never return.

Leak 02

Checkout drop-offs on e-commerce

Your shop requires 12 checkout steps and fails during 3D-Secure bank OTP timeouts. Nigerian buyers demand instant WhatsApp or automated bank transfer verification.

Leak 03

The contact form dead-end

Inbound quotes get buried in an unmonitored inbox. By the time a sales rep replies 18 hours later, the client has already signed with a faster competitor.

Leak 04

Mobile lag on local data networks

Heavy uncompressed media that takes 8 seconds to load on MTN or Airtel. Over 70% of your mobile traffic bounces before the first section renders.

Leak 05

Zero commercial Google visibility

Your site exists, but when high-intent buyers in Lagos search for your exact service, only your competitors appear on page one.

How long does a website take to build?

Standard corporate and marketing websites typically take 3 to 6 weeks from strategy sign-off to live launch. Complex e-commerce and web applications range between 6 and 10 weeks depending on custom integrations.

Do you provide maintenance and technical support after launch?

Yes. Every CKDIGITAL project includes post-launch testing, warranty support, and optional ongoing maintenance plans covering security updates, speed monitoring, backups, and content updates.

Can our internal staff update the website content?

Absolutely. We build with intuitive content management systems (WordPress and Shopify). Upon launch, we provide video walkthroughs and staff training so your team can edit text, upload products, and post updates without writing code.
