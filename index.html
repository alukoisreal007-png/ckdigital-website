What we build

Five automated systems that run your operations while you sleep.

1. WhatsApp Speed-to-Lead (< 30s)

What it fixes: Missed night sales and slow replies during peak rush hours.

How it works: Greets every inquiry instantly, provides price lists and sizes, filters out tire-kickers, and passes ready buyers directly to live staff.

2. Instant Bank Transfer Verification

What it fixes: Fake alert screenshots and hours spent checking bank mobile apps.

How it works: Generates a dedicated virtual account for that specific order. The moment funds transfer, payment verifies in 5 seconds and generates an official receipt.

3. The 3-Day Anti-Ghosting Follow-Up

What it fixes: Customers who ask for a quote and disappear.

How it works: Triggers friendly, non-spammy check-ins on Day 2 and Day 4 with social proof or limited inventory notices. Recovers up to 35% of lost quotes.

What it fixes: Copying addresses by hand into WhatsApp dispatch groups and misrouted orders.

How it works: The minute payment is verified, the order pushes to your dispatch riders, and your customer gets a live tracking link.

What it fixes: Selling items you don't have and losing half a day building weekly Excel reports.

How it works: Syncs stock between your physical store and digital catalog in real time. Sends the owner one clear WhatsApp morning briefing every day.
